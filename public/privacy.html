Privacy notice

Last updated 20 October 2026

Who I am

This is the personal portfolio website of Mohammad Shaikh, an individual developer based in Liverpool, United Kingdom. I am the data controller for this site. Contact: shaikh.mohammad1099@gmail.com.

What this site collects

This site has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and sets no cookies. The only data handled is listed below.

1. Chat assistant

The "Ask anything about my work" assistant sends the text you type to a serverless function I run on Vercel, which forwards it to an AI model provider (OpenRouter) to generate an answer about my portfolio. Messages are processed to answer your question and are not saved by me. Please do not enter personal, financial or sensitive information in the chat. Lawful basis: legitimate interest (answering questions about my work). Vercel and OpenRouter are based in the United States; transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses. A basic per-IP rate limit is applied in memory to prevent abuse and is not retained.

2. Contact form

The contact form does not send anything to this site. Pressing send opens your own email application with the message pre-filled; the message is only sent if you send it from there, and only to my email address (hosted by Google). I keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to reply and follow up. Lawful basis: your request / legitimate interest.

3. Flappy game and local storage

The arcade game keeps your best score and chosen nickname in your own browser so they are there next time. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to email a score to me. You can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

4. Hosting and fonts

The site is served by Hostinger (virtualuniverso.co.uk) and GitHub Pages. Like any web host they log IP addresses and request details for security and reliability for a short period. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font files from Google and Google sees your IP address; no cookies are set by that request.
